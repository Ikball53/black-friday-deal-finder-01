How we decide if it is really a deal

  • Real deal — a new low or the lowest price seen this season, with the evidence on the card.
  • Strong price — a genuinely good cut, but not a record low.
  • Routine sale — a small cut on a product that rarely drops much; fine if you need it now, not a reason to rush.

Every discount is computed from the sale price and the usual price the store or source showed — never typed in. A “was” price that looks inflated is called out on the card instead of being trusted. Prices change fast in Black Friday week: always confirm the price on the store page before you buy.
